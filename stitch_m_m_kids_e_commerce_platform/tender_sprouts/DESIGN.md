## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
The design system reflects the tender joy, innocence, and conscious care of modern parenting. Built for a premium yet approachable Vietnamese children's wear brand (ages 3–12), the interface evokes feelings of reassurance, warmth, playful delight, and reliable craftsmanship. The visual universe must feel safe, gentle, and unhurried—like a sunny morning in a garden nursery. It addresses mindful parents who prioritize organic comfort, durability, and contemporary aesthetic taste without visual clutter.

### Visual Language & Aesthetic Direction
The style merges **Organic Tactile Minimalism** with **Playful Soft-Modernism**:
- **Soft Geometry**: Expansive, cloud-like rounded corners (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl` and full pills) remove rigid, institutional sharpness.
- **Warm Airiness**: Ample white and cream breathing room ensures high-end editorial clarity, avoiding the chaotic, neon sensory overload often associated with children's products.
- **Subtle Tactility**: Micro-interactions provide soft, springy, pillow-like tactile responses (`scale(0.98)` active states, pillowy shadows) that feel friendly to parents and captivating to kids browsing alongside them.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from early botanical life, natural linen, and gentle sunlight. All tones maintain a warm undertone to preserve an inviting, human presence throughout the commerce experience.

### Palette Roles & Balance
- **Primary (`#4E8773` - Sage Sprout)**: Represents health, natural organic cotton, and renewal. Anchors key interactive landmarks, checkout primary CTAs, active filters, and essential brand identity touchpoints.
- **Secondary (`#E8927C` - Soft Rose Coral)**: Provides warmth, affectionate energy, and emotional resonance. Used for high-engagement elements, wishlists, celebratory promotions, and playful category badges.
- **Tertiary (`#E8C568` - Buttercup Yellow)**: Evokes morning sunlight and innocence. Used selectively for review stars, size recommendation tags, sale ribbons, and delightful micro-accents.
- **Neutral Core (`#787670` - Mineral Pebble)**: A warm stone gray avoiding cold blue undertones. Keeps product details legible, sophisticated, and soothing.

### Surface System
- **Canvas Base**: `#FDFBF7` (Warm Linen Cream) replaces clinical white `#FFFFFF` to reduce eye strain and give a soft, cotton fabric feel.
- **Card Surface**: Pure `#FFFFFF` floating softly atop `#FDFBF7` to create organic distinction without harsh lines.
- **Subtle Container**: `#F3EFE6` (Oat Silk) for search inputs, size guide backdrops, and unselected filter chips.

## Typography

### Font Pairing Strategy
- **Headlines & Interactive Labels (`Quicksand`)**: Its naturally rounded terminal shapes create instant warmth, kinship, and innocence without devolving into childish novelty. It reads clearly at large and medium scales while communicating gentle protection.
- **Body Text & Tabular Data (`Nunito Sans`)**: Balances the rounded geometric nature of Quicksand with open counters, exceptional x-height, and optimal Vietnamese diacritic clarity. It provides high reading comfort during material composition reviews, sizing charts, and checkout forms.

### Rules of Usage
- Never use all-caps on body text or headlines longer than 3 words.
- All uppercase styling is restricted exclusively to micro-labels (`label-sm`), such as age tags (`3-4Y`, `NEWBORN`), with generous letter spacing to avoid density.
- Diacritics in Vietnamese titles require at least `1.25x` to `1.35x` line-height breathing room to avoid accent collision.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Model
The layout follows an adaptive fluid grid within bounded containers, prioritized for mobile-first parental browsing (often one-handed on mobile devices).

- **Desktop (1200px and up)**: 12-column grid with a maximum content canvas of `1280px`. Gutter is set to `1.5rem` (24px) with safe margin `2.5rem` (40px). Product grids adopt 4 columns.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8-column grid with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `1.5rem` (24px) margins. Product catalogs adapt to 3 columns.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `0.75rem` (12px) gutters and `1rem` (16px) outer margins. Product lists display in a 2-column card grid with stacked action tags.

### Spacing Rhythm
Spacing is calibrated strictly to an 8pt structural rhythm (with a 4pt baseline for micro-adjustments). Spacious vertical rhythm prevents visual friction, ensuring that imagery of children's garments feels breathable, airy, and high-end.

## Elevation & Depth

### Ambient Shadow Architecture
Traditional pitch-black shadows (`rgba(0,0,0,...)`) are forbidden. All elevation layers utilize **warm tinted diffusion**, borrowing colors from the warm stone neutral and sage green palette. This produces a soft, floating effect similar to morning sun illuminating a nursery.

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground)**: Base background `#FDFBF7`. Borders are nonexistent or faint organic lines using `#EFEADF`.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Product Tiles, Form Wells)**:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(110, 105, 95, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(110, 105, 95, 0.03);`
  Creates a tender lift off the cream ground while retaining soft contours.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards, Dropdowns, Segment Pickers)**:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 24px -4px rgba(78, 135, 115, 0.10), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(110, 105, 95, 0.04);`
  Infuses an imperceptible green tint on hover, visually affirming interactivity.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Add-to-Cart Bar, Modals, Quick-View Sheets)**:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(90, 85, 75, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(90, 85, 75, 0.06);`

### Glassmorphism Treatment
For floating headers, sticky category navigation, and quick mobile navigation bars, utilize soft frosted glass:
`backdrop-filter: blur(16px); background: rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.85);` with an ultra-thin lower separation line of `rgba(215, 208, 195, 0.4)`.

## Shapes

### Shape Language & Geometry
Sharp corners do not exist in this system. The corner architecture utilizes soft hyper-ellipses (squircle logic) and pill capsules to communicate warmth, physical safety, and playfulness.

- **Interactive Pills (`rounded-full` / `9999px`)**: Primary buttons, quick filter chips, age badges, and search pills.
- **Hero Containers & Feature Cards (`rounded-3xl` / `24px - 32px`)**: Product display hero sections, promotional banners, lookbook carousels, and bottom sheets.
- **Standard Cards & Input Surfaces (`rounded-2xl` / `16px - 20px`)**: Standard product listing cards, review containers, quantity pickers, and modal windows.
- **Micro Elements (`rounded-lg` / `8px - 12px`)**: Swatches, notification dots, and inner icon wrappers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid Sage Sprout (`#4E8773`) fill with pure white text (`#FFFFFF`). Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `14px 28px`. On hover, color transitions smoothly to `#3F7160` with a subtle elevation shift. Active state applies `scale(0.98)` to simulate an organic cushion press.
- **Secondary Button**: Outlined in 1.5px `#4E8773` with `#FDFBF7` surface and `#4E8773` text. On hover, fills with 8% Sage Sprout tint (`#EEF4F1`).
- **Tertiary / Soft Button**: Warm peach or soft cream background (`#F9EAE5` or `#F3EFE6`) with matching contrast text (`#C66650` or `#4E8773`). Used for cart quick-add or age-filter toggles.

### Chips & Badges
- **Age-Selector Chips (e.g., "3-4 Tuổi", "5-6 Tuổi")**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), borderless `#F3EFE6` in unselected state. Selected state transitions to `#4E8773` with white text and an ambient glow.
- **Product Highlight Badges**: Small capsules (`rounded-full`, padding `4px 10px`, typography `label-sm`).
  - *Organic Cotton*: Soft Sage background (`#E8F1ED`), Sage text (`#356555`).
  - *Bán Chạy (Best Seller)*: Soft Peach background (`#FBECE8`), Rose text (`#C66650`).
  - *Mới (New)*: Warm Butter background (`#FAF4DE`), Gold Ochre text (`#8F7326`).

### Cards (Product & Story)
- **Product Card**: White canvas surface (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-2xl`, Level 1 ambient shadow. Overflow hidden with an aspect ratio of `4:5` for garment imagery. Images possess an ultra-subtle warm background wash. 
- A circular "Favorite" heart button floats top-right (`40px x 40px`, white translucent blur, `rounded-full`).
- Price display: Current price in bold `#4E8773` or `#2E3330`; original strikethrough price in `#9E9B93`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: `height: 48px`, `rounded-full` or `rounded-2xl`, background `#F6F3EC`, border 1.5px transparent. Focused state triggers border color `#4E8773` with a soft outer ring `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(78, 135, 115, 0.15);` and white background fill.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: `22px x 22px` rounded squares (`rounded-lg` for checkboxes, `rounded-full` for radios). Unchecked: 1.5px `#D4CEC3` border. Checked: filled `#4E8773` with white organic checkmark icon.

### Specialized Children's Wear Components
- **Size & Growth Predictor Bar**: A soft horizontal slider allowing Vietnamese mothers to adjust child height/weight, dynamically highlighting the recommended size chip (`Size 100`, `Size 110`, etc.) in Buttercup Yellow with an encouraging growth note ("Bé mặc thoải mái đến hè").
- **Fabric Feel Indicator**: A row of three pill badges indicating *Co giãn nhẹ (Slight stretch)*, *100% Organic*, and *Thoáng khí (Breathable)*, accompanied by soft line illustrations.
